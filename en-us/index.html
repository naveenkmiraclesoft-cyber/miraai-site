miraAI is Miracle Software Systems' enterprise generative AI platform for coordinating knowledge, data, models, and agentic workflows.

miraAI is designed for integration-led delivery. Supported systems, connector depth, and implementation responsibilities are confirmed during solution design.

The platform supports cloud, hybrid, and on-premises implementation patterns. The final architecture depends on business, security, and infrastructure requirements.

The platform is designed to support role-aware access, workflow traceability, evaluation, and deployment controls. Specific controls are documented for each implementation.
