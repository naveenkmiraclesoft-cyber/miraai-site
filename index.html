What we do best

The Centralized Intelligence Platform

miraAI is an enterprise-ready Generative AI framework by Miracle Software Systems that unifies prompts, workflows, and data into a single layer, eliminating silos for scalable AI deployment. It accelerates business innovation with built-in traceability, drift detection, and evaluation to ensure reliable AI outcomes across clouds like Azure, AWS, and GCP.

Orchestration

Agent Sphere

Connects Core, Data, and Knowledge Spheres — routes complex queries, merges results into clear answers, and provides reusable agents across projects.

RAG & Documents

Knowledge Sphere

Interact with your documents and external sources (Azure Blob, GCP Buckets, AWS S3). Extracts text, chunks content, creates embeddings in a vector database, and uses LLMs to answer your queries in a chat interface.

Text-to-SQL

Data Sphere

Query your databases in plain language and instantly get structured data — enabling faster, data-driven decisions without coding. Turn raw data into clear, actionable insights for everyone.

AI assistant

Core Sphere

Works like an AI assistant — when a user asks a question, it connects to the selected model, retrieves the response, and delivers it instantly. Quick, accurate answers from the model of your choice.

Multi-model

Multi-Model Flexibility

Align AI models precisely to your workflows and business objectives.
